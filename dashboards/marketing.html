Method

Five questions, one notebook

Q1

Sentiment

RoBERTa model trained on tweets, with VADER as a robustness check (r = 0.54).

Q2

Topic

Zero-shot classification: informative, entertaining or promotional.

Q3

Emojis

Emoji counts, types and signature emojis per brand.

Q4

Drivers

Negative binomial regression on likes and comments (overdispersed counts).

Q5

Brand differences

Separate models per brand plus interaction effects.

Data: course dataset of 600 posts (200 per brand) for three brands. Model outputs come from the project notebook; engagement and emoji figures are computed from the raw posts. Interaction effects were small and inconsistent across brands, so they are not shown here.
