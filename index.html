Bay Area Bike Share: demand, stations & weather

A Power BI report on 354K bike-share trips across 76 stations in five Bay Area cities (Sep 2014 – Aug 2015), combined with daily weather, ending in insights and rebalancing recommendations. Explore it below: filters and cross-highlighting work as in Power BI.

Data model

Star schema with a marked date table; each trip is split into a departure and an arrival event so every station shows bikes leaving and arriving.

DAX

Unique-trip counting, month-over-month change, net bike flow per station and its 60-day moving average with a 7-day forecast.

Power Query

Parameterized CSV sources, locale-safe parsing of dates and numbers, and imperial-to-metric conversion of the weather data.
